# Sources & Citations

Market data and competitor analyses backing [Competitive Landscape](../01-product/competitive-landscape.md) and [Vision](../00-project/vision.md) § Problem.

- **Timeleft** (social dining app) — "Meet new people in your area... connect over dinner, a coffee date." Shows the global trend toward intent-based social meetups, but lacks professional verification and Sri Lanka support. [timeleft.com](https://timeleft.com/)
- **Bumble Bizz** (professional swiping) — "Bumble has just launched Bumble Bizz, a mode which lets users professionally network as they swipe." Proves demand for mobile professional networking, but relies on static profiles rather than real-time context. [Bumble](https://bumble.com/en-us/bizz) · [Mashable coverage](https://mashable.com/article/bumble-bizz-networking-feature)
- **sRide** (corporate carpooling) — "sRide is the trusted carpooling app for professionals who want to save money... make their daily commute count." Validates demand for verified corporate ride-sharing, but has no social/networking intent layer. [sride.co](https://sride.co/) · [Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=co.sride&hl=en&pli=1)
- **Lunchclub** (AI video networking) — "Lunchclub is an AI superconnector that makes introductions for 1:1 video meetings." Demonstrates AI-matching success for professionals, but restricted to virtual interactions. [lunchclub.com](https://lunchclub.com/)
- **Meetup** (Sri Lanka professional groups) — "Find Meetup events so you can do more of what matters to you." Shows existing demand for professional networking in Sri Lanka specifically, but relies on scheduled, large-group events rather than instant 1-on-1 matching. [Meetup — Sri Lanka professional networking](https://www.meetup.com/topics/professional-networking/lk/)
