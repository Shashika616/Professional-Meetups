# Trust Levels

Users are not treated equally — access is gated by a discrete trust level plus a continuous trust score layered on top (see [Trust & Safety Architecture](../03-architecture/trust-and-safety-architecture.md) § Continuous Trust Score). Recorded as a deliberate design choice in [ADR-002](../04-decisions/adr-002-multi-level-continuous-trust-scoring.md).

**Why this exists at all**: this app connects working professionals with strangers for real, in-person meetings — coffee, drinks, a concert, a ride — precisely the kind of casual social contact that's easy for a fraudster, thief, or manipulator to exploit. We are responsible for every person's safety the moment we introduce them to someone. Trust levels are the mechanism for that responsibility: nobody gets access to matching with strangers without first clearing a bar we control, and the bar gets higher as the intent gets riskier.

**Revised 2026-08-16** ([ADR-006](../04-decisions/adr-006-progressive-linkedin-first-trust-onboarding.md)) to a progressive, LinkedIn-first onboarding model — this supersedes the original "three mandatory pillars" framing. See [ADR-006](../04-decisions/adr-006-progressive-linkedin-first-trust-onboarding.md) for the full rationale.

**Revised again 2026-08-19** ([ADR-014](../04-decisions/adr-014-federated-base-identity-optional-linkedin-age-eligibility.md), final shape after direct design discussion): account creation now has **three parallel, co-equal paths** — Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, or email+password — all landing at Level 0, plus LinkedIn as a fourth path that grants Level 1 immediately. Driven by a real Apple Guideline 4.8 compliance gap (LinkedIn-exclusive account creation isn't allowed) and by not every user wanting to federate through Apple/Google. See [ADR-014](../04-decisions/adr-014-federated-base-identity-optional-linkedin-age-eligibility.md) for the full reasoning, including its Identity Resolution design (how the system tells these paths apart without creating duplicate or hijacked accounts) and one recommendation still open for confirmation: retiring Level 1b now that three other paths already unblock account creation without it.

**Age eligibility ([ADR-014](../04-decisions/adr-014-federated-base-identity-optional-linkedin-age-eligibility.md)):** a mandatory, blocking 18+ self-attestation (checkbox, no date of birth collected) gates all four sign-up paths uniformly, including LinkedIn direct signup — this is an eligibility gate on account creation, not a graduated trust step. Self-attestation only; not a substitute for the "reasonable methods" adult-confirmation some regions (and Google Play's dating-app rules) increasingly require — tracked as a known gap in [App Store & Play Store Compliance](../07-research/app-store-and-play-store-compliance.md).

## Level 0 — Base Identity, Read-Only

**Concrete since [ADR-014](../04-decisions/adr-014-federated-base-identity-optional-linkedin-age-eligibility.md) (2026-08-19)** — previously just "installed the app / mid-signup," now a real account, reachable via any of: Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, or email+password (OTP-verified email required at signup, reusing Level 2's existing OTP mechanism — see [Verification Model](verification-model.md)), plus the mandatory age-eligibility confirmation above. **Permanently zero-trust**: linking any combination of Apple/Google to an account never raises trust level, no matter how many are linked — they prove device/platform identity, not professional identity, which is what this ladder actually measures. Allowed: view the app shell, browse open Meetups read-only (this capability moves down from what 1a previously held exclusively). Not allowed: message, host or request to join a Meetup, appear in others' discovery, complete a full profile beyond basic display info.

## Level 1 — LinkedIn Connected

LinkedIn is the **only** path from Level 0 to Level 1 — reachable two ways: as a fourth sign-up button that grants Level 1 immediately (unchanged from [ADR-011](../04-decisions/adr-011-linkedin-onboarding-slice-design.md)'s original design), or connected later from the Profile page to bump an existing Level 0 account up. Two verification paths, not treated as equally trustworthy:

- **1a — Federated (preferred).** LinkedIn OAuth/OpenID login. The platform confirms the user actually controls the LinkedIn account. Allowed, on top of Level 0: complete full profile, limited discovery within low-risk intents only (coffee, lunch, networking), basic messaging with heavy new-account restrictions (see [Trust & Safety Architecture](../03-architecture/trust-and-safety-architecture.md) § New-account slowdown). **Required before Level 2 is reachable**, regardless of which of the four paths created the account — professional-identity confirmation stays the prerequisite for the phone/email/personal-detail step, preserving this app's professional-identity-first premise. A connected LinkedIn shows a "LinkedIn Verified" badge on the profile, paired with this page's own Design Note caveat below.
- **1b — Claimed (fallback, "not secure").** User pastes their LinkedIn profile URL instead of connecting via OAuth. This is a **self-reported claim, not a verification**: anyone can paste any public URL. **Product rule: Level 1b accounts cannot match or be matched with other users.** **Recommended for retirement ([ADR-014](../04-decisions/adr-014-federated-base-identity-optional-linkedin-age-eligibility.md), open for confirmation)**: 1b existed only to unblock account creation when OAuth wasn't available — three other paths now do that, so 1b's original justification is gone.

**Duplicate accounts across the four paths are accepted, not prevented** ([ADR-014](../04-decisions/adr-014-federated-base-identity-optional-linkedin-age-eligibility.md)): if the same person signs up once via Apple and separately via Google with no shared verified identifier between the two, that's genuinely undetectable and not engineered around. The mitigation is that every account, however many exist, is independently capped by its own trust level — duplicates can't buy functionality, only real verification can.

Restricted at all of Level 0-1: no dating, no ride sharing, no high-risk intents, no search by company, no bulk visibility.

## Level 2 — Personal Verified

Adds, on top of Level 1a: phone OTP verification, personal email verification, and personal details (legal name, address) captured and verified — see [Verification Model](verification-model.md). **This is the real floor for interacting with strangers.** Allowed: standard matching and messaging, medium-risk intents (coffee, lunch, drinks, casual hangouts, event/concert companionship), [Domain Model](domain-model.md) ([ADR-015](../04-decisions/adr-015-post-meetup-star-ratings.md)), community participation.

**Concrete gate, added 2026-08-17 ([ADR-013](../04-decisions/adr-013-host-initiated-meetup-scheduling-with-join-requests.md)):** hosting or requesting to join a real-world Meetup ([Domain Model](domain-model.md) § Meetup) requires Level 2 for coffee/lunch/networking/mentorship, Level 4 for ride-share/dating — `IntentType.requiredTrustLevel`'s default floor moved from 1 to 2 for this reason. Level 0 and 1a can both browse/view open Meetups (consistent with [ADR-006](../04-decisions/adr-006-progressive-linkedin-first-trust-onboarding.md)'s low-friction-signup goal) but neither can host one or send a join request.

## Level 3 — Corporate Verified

Optional trust booster on top of Level 2: verified corporate/work email ([Verification Model](verification-model.md) § Professional email verification), or — for B2B customers — verification via company SSO, corporate directory, HR-approved employee list, or enterprise admin verification. Not required to use the app, but unlocks: search-by-company, corporate community features, internal networking, safe commuting, cross-team collaboration, and is a strong positive trust-score signal. This is the tier the Enterprise revenue tier depends on — see [Revenue Model](../01-product/revenue-model.md).

## Level 4 — KYC / High-Trust (optional, introduce if/when needed)

Not required for MVP. For users who want, or the platform requires for the highest-risk intents (dating, ride sharing, late-night or private meetups), a KYC-style check proving the account belongs to a real, live human: government ID verification, face liveness check ("live pics"), video verification, optional address proof, background check where legally allowed, driver/vehicle verification for ride sharing. **This should stay optional and privacy-sensitive** — no sensitive documents collected unless necessary, encrypted, legally compliant, and securely stored (see [Privacy & Anti-Abuse Controls](../03-architecture/privacy-and-anti-abuse-controls.md)). Revisit making this mandatory before enabling dating or open ride-sharing ([ADR-004](../04-decisions/adr-004-defer-dating-and-open-ride-sharing.md)).

## Design note

A verified badge should never be read by users as "this person is safe" — only "this account passed certain checks." Always pair verification UI with the caveat that verification does not guarantee good intentions. See [Safety Features Catalog](../03-architecture/safety-features-catalog.md) § Do Not Over-Trust "Verified" Badges.
