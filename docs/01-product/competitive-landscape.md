# Competitive Landscape

The market gap this product exploits — see [Vision](../00-project/vision.md) § Problem for the summary and [Sources & Citations](../07-research/sources-and-citations.md) for the underlying quotes/links.

| Competitor | What it does | Why it's not this product |
|---|---|---|
| **Timeleft** | Social dining — "meet new people in your area... connect over dinner, a coffee date." | No professional verification; no Sri Lanka support. |
| **Bumble Bizz** | Professional swiping/networking mode. | Static profiles, not real-time/context-aware matching. |
| **sRide** | Corporate carpooling. | No social or mentorship layer. |
| **Lunchclub** | AI-matched 1:1 video meetings. | Virtual only — no real-world serendipity. |
| **Meetup** | Scheduled, large-group professional events (has an active Sri Lanka professional-networking category). | Scheduled/group format, not instant 1-on-1 matching. |

## The gap

No platform combines: verified professional identity + real-world/location context + instant intent-based matching + safety infrastructure suitable for meeting strangers in person. Each competitor owns one axis (professional trust, real-time location, or social intent) but not all three at once.
