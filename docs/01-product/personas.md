# Personas

Part of [Requirements](requirements.md) § Target audience.

## The Overworked Professional

Works long corporate hours and has little time or organic opportunity to build a social life outside of work. Wants company for something low-key and human — coffee, a drink after work, a concert, a hangout — but has no safe way to find it: dating apps aren't the right fit for "I just want someone to grab a drink with," and there's no trustworthy way to meet a stranger for something that casual. This is the persona the whole trust-level and safety system exists to serve: they need real confidence that the person on the other end of the match has a verifiable professional identity (LinkedIn, ideally a corporate email) before they'll ever agree to meet up. Primary user of the coffee/lunch/drinks/event-companionship intents; the most direct validation of [Vision](../00-project/vision.md) § The core scenario.

## The Commuter

Drives or takes transit daily; wants to share a ride or grab coffee with a peer in the same industry. Primary consumer of the ride-sharing and coffee/lunch intents — see [Safety Features Catalog](../03-architecture/safety-features-catalog.md) § Ride-Sharing Safety for why this persona's safety needs are treated as high-risk.

## The New Relocator

Moved to a new city (e.g. Colombo) and wants to find verified professional friends without resorting to dating apps. Core validation for the "verified professional, not a dating app" positioning in [Vision](../00-project/vision.md).

## The Founder

Looking for spontaneous brainstorming sessions or mentorship over lunch. Primary user of the mentorship and networking intents.

## The Corporate HR

Wants to facilitate safe commuting and cross-departmental networking for employees. Primary user of the Enterprise tier — see [Revenue Model](revenue-model.md) and [Safety Features Catalog](../03-architecture/safety-features-catalog.md) § Enterprise Mode.
